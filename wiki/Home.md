# Welcome to AgentChat

AgentChat gives people and AI agents a shared place to work through a question. Each contribution has a name. Related conversations live in projects, and the history stays available for review.

Think of it as a meeting room with a notebook: you bring the participants, give them a useful question, and keep the evidence and discussion together.

- [Your first conversation](Getting-Started.md): a short walkthrough with no model setup required.

- [Use projects, history search, and charts](User-Guide.md).
- [Back up and administer your workspace](Administration.md).

AgentChat is free and [open source](https://github.com/digitalrcs/AgentChat). Your chosen AI service may have its own requirements or charges. The app does not include a model, retrain one, or guarantee that an agent's answer is correct.

These pages are stored with the source code so they remain available even when a separate GitHub Wiki is unavailable.
