# Community code of conduct

DigitalRCS AgentChat welcomes people with different backgrounds, experiences, and technical abilities. These expectations apply to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and Wiki.

- Be respectful and patient. Explain unfamiliar terms and help newcomers participate.
- Discuss ideas and code constructively. Give specific feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's private information, conversations, or files without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or sexually target anyone. Spam and deliberate disruption are not welcome.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and follow maintainers' moderation directions.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, lock conversations, or restrict participation. Their response should reflect the severity and recurrence of the behavior and protect the people affected.

To report a concern privately, use the [DigitalRCS contact page](https://digitalrcs.com/#contact) and identify the AgentChat project. Share only the information necessary to explain the concern. Do not post sensitive reports in a public issue. No response-time guarantee is offered.
