Start small. Add another perspective.

Install DigitalRCS AgentChat on your Windows computer, add your name, and open your first conversation. You can connect local AI models when you are ready.

  • Download the latest release
  • Run Setup, then Start
  • Open your local workspace
Open the installation guide

Know what it does.

  • No subscription for DigitalRCS AgentChat. Your chosen AI services may have their own costs.
  • Your history stays in your local workspace. You choose which agents receive it.
  • Search past discussions. This gives agents context; it does not retrain their models.
